June 14 · A letter for you

Dear Khondamir,

Nineteen years ago today, the world got a little brighter, and it has been getting brighter ever since.

This capsule kept one quiet promise until this moment: to remind you how far you have come. Every late night, every small win, every lesson you never asked for has shaped the person reading this.

Nineteen is a doorway. Walk through it with curiosity. Be brave with your dreams, gentle with yourself, and generous with the people who love you.

Whatever this year holds, I hope it surprises you in the best ways.

Happy birthday ✨
With love, always in your corner.
